Service 04

Apostille & document authentication

Documents bound for use in Hague-convention countries — and many that aren't — require an apostille from the issuing state. Virginia issues apostilles through the Secretary of the Commonwealth in Richmond. We hand-walk your documents through, sparing you weeks of mail turnaround.

  • ✓
    Personal documentsBirth, marriage, death certificates; diplomas; FBI background checks.
  • ✓
    Corporate documentsArticles of incorporation, certificates of good standing, board resolutions, powers of attorney.
  • ✓
    TranslationsCertified translations notarized for international use.
  • ✓
    Embassy & consulate add-onsFor non-Hague countries, we coordinate the additional embassy authentication step.

Typical turnaround: 2–5 business days standard, same-day rush available with courier service.

Service 05

Remote Online Notarization (RON)

Virginia was an early adopter of remote online notarization, and the law has held up. RON sessions happen entirely over secure audio-video, with knowledge-based identity verification and a tamper-evident e-seal. Recognized in all 50 states.

  • ✓
    Anywhere, any time zoneIdeal for clients overseas, deployed military, or anyone who simply can't travel.
  • ✓
    Same legal weightRON acts have the same effect as in-person notarization in Virginia.
  • ✓
    Suitable for most documentsSome documents (notably wills) still require in-person — we'll tell you up front.
