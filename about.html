Meet Your Notary

James Parascand

Virginia-commissioned notary public · NNA-certified signing agent · medical-notary specialist.

Founder & Notary

The notary you can call at any hour.

Parascand Notary was built around a simple idea: when documents need to be signed, the moments are often hard. A loved one in the ICU. A real-estate deadline that won't move. A diploma that has to be in Madrid by Friday.

Those moments deserve a professional who shows up on time, knows the protocols, treats every signer with dignity, and gets it right. That's the standard James Parascand built the practice around — and the standard every appointment is held to.

Based in Virginia, with active service across Richmond and the Tri-Cities, all of Northern Virginia, and the Charlottesville area. Available 24/7 for medical and emergency situations; standard mobile and signing work scheduled to fit your day.
