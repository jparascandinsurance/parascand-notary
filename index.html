Common Questions

Quick answers

The four questions clients ask most. See the full FAQ →

Do you come to hospitals and nursing homes?
Yes — bedside and medical notarizations are our specialty. We serve hospitals, hospice agencies, assisted living facilities, and nursing homes throughout Virginia, including same-day and after-hours visits for time-sensitive documents like medical powers of attorney and advance directives.
What areas of Virginia do you serve?
Richmond and the Tri-Cities (Petersburg, Hopewell, Colonial Heights), all of Northern Virginia (Fairfax, Arlington, Alexandria, Loudoun, Reston, Tysons), and the Charlottesville area. Travel beyond these zones is available by quote.
Are you available 24/7?
Yes — 24/7 emergency and on-call service, including nights, weekends, and holidays. Hospitals discharge at all hours and time-sensitive legal documents do not wait.
What does a mobile notary cost?
Virginia caps notarial acts at $10 each. Travel and convenience fees are separate and depend on distance, urgency, and complexity. Most general appointments run $50–$100 all-in. Loan signings start at $125. Apostille runs $150 and up. Call or message for an exact quote.
